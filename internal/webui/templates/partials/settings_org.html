Write-only: values are encrypted at rest (AES-GCM) and can never be read back. A secret resolves in the repositories its policy selects for a trusted run; a repository secret of the same name wins, and an environment secret wins over both. Fork pull requests never receive any. Logs, annotations and step summaries are masked best effort.
