An environment is a deployment target a job names (environment:). Required reviewers hold a deployment pending until one of them (or a repo admin) approves it — nothing starts and no environment secret is resolved before that. Secrets scoped here are injected only into an approved job that names this environment, and never into a fork run. A reviewer needs at least write access.

{{else}} {{if not .Enabled}}
Environment secrets are unavailable.

Set server.secrets_key (or server.auth.session_secret) to enable at-rest encryption. Environments and reviewers still work.

{{end}}

Comma-separated principals with write access or higher. Leave empty for no approval gate. At most six reviewers and teams together.

Comma-separated teams as org/team. Each team must exist; its members at decision time must approve.

0 disables it. The timer starts when a deployment is created and holds it before it can start (max 360 minutes).

Comma-separated patterns (exact names and *). Empty allows every ref; a deployment from any other ref fails closed.
